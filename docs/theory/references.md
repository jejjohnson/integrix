# References

## Quadrature

1. Davis, P. J., & Rabinowitz, P. (1984). *Methods of Numerical Integration* (2nd ed.). Academic Press. Interpolatory rules, the moment equations, Newton–Cotes and Gauss rules (chapter 2).
2. Trefethen, L. N. (2008). Is Gauss quadrature better than Clenshaw–Curtis? *SIAM Review* 50(1), 67–87. The geometric rate of Gauss and Clenshaw–Curtis for analytic integrands (chapter 3).
3. Trefethen, L. N., & Weideman, J. A. C. (2014). The exponentially convergent trapezoidal rule. *SIAM Review* 56(3), 385–458. The trapezoid rule on periodic data and Euler–Maclaurin (chapter 3).

## Numerical methods in geophysics

4. Durran, D. R. (2010). *Numerical Methods for Fluid Dynamics: With Applications to Geophysics* (2nd ed.). Springer. Spectral transform models on Gaussian grids, and finite-volume methods (chapter 3).
